1. Solar Radiation Model

Q_solar = α × G × A  [W]
sin(alt) = sin(lat)·sin(dec) + cos(lat)·cos(dec)·cos(hra)
  • Spencer (1971) solar declination and altitude algorithm
  • Direct beam projection + isotropic diffuse sky view factor

2. Conductive Heat Transfer

U = 1 / (1/h_i + Σ(L/k) + 1/h_o)  [W/m²·K]
Q_cond = U × A × (T_out - T_in)   [W]
  • ASHRAE standard boundary layers: $h_i = 8.0, h_o = 25.0$ W/m²·K
  • Positive sign convention: Heat entering shelter

3. Convective Heat Transfer

h_vert = 10 + 4v   [W/m²·K]
h_horiz = 10 + 6v  [W/m²·K]
  • Wind-speed ($v$) dependent correlation coefficients
  • Vertical walls vs horizontal roof orientations

4. Radiative Heat Transfer

Q_rad = ε × σ × A × (T_s⁴ - T_sur⁴)  [W]
σ = 5.67×10⁻⁸ W/(m²·K⁴)
  • Stefan-Boltzmann absolute temperature fourth-power law
  • Night-sky radiative cooling dissipation

5. Thermal Capacitance (Dynamics)

dT/dt = Q_net / (C_total × ρ)
C_total = Mass_total × c_specific  [J/K]
  • Thermal lag damping interior temperature swings
  • 1-hour integration timesteps for 24-hour cycle

6. ASHRAE 55 PMV/PPD Comfort

T_op = f_r·T_mrt + (1-f_r)·T_air
PMV = (0.303·e^(-0.036·M) + 0.028)·(T_op - 22)
PPD = 100 - 95·e^-(0.03353·PMV⁴ + 0.2179·PMV²)
  • Comfort zone: $22^\circ\text{C} \le T_{op} \le 26^\circ\text{C}$ AND $\text{PPD} < 20\%$
  • Minimum physiological dissatisfaction: 5.0%

Physics of the Nocturnal Temperature Plunge & Standalone Solution

Why traditional uninsulated shelters freeze at night and how area-specific passive design maintains 24/7 comfort.

❌ The Existing Problem in Ladakh Shelters

  • Daytime Trapping: High solar irradiance warms the shelter during sunshine hours (~7.9 hrs), but heat is stored only in thin surfaces.
  • Post-Sunset Plunge: After 17:00, ambient temperatures plummet toward -12°C. Thin corrugated GI roofs and uninsulated masonry exhibit high U-values ($U > 3.5\text{ W/m}^2\text{K}$).
  • Glazing & Draft Losses: Single glass windows and door drafts leak up to 65% of interior heat through conduction and infiltration ($ACH > 1.5$).
  • Fossil Fuel Demand: Forces occupants to burn 15–20 Litres of polluting kerosene / coal per day for survival.

✅ The Developed Standalone Passive Solution

  • Direct South Solar Façade: Elongated East-West aspect ratio (1.6:1) oriented True South with double Low-E solar collector glazing ($SHGC = 0.76$).
  • High-Mass Thermal Storage: 350mm Rammed Earth / Dense Adobe Trombe wall (65,000 kg sensible mass) storing solar heat during the day.
  • Post-Sunset Decoupling: Heavy multi-layer insulated night shutters ($R = 2.0\text{ m}^2\text{K/W}$) deployed at sunset across all glazed openings.
  • Continuous Thermal Envelope: 200mm PUF/composite roof ($U = 0.12\text{ W/m}^2\text{K}$) ensuring zero thermal bridging.
